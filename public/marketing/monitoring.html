How GPS IoT Monitoring Works

1. NGO Field Check-Ins

Field workers use mobile app to check-in at project sites. GPS coordinates verified against registered locations.

2. Geotagged Documentation

Photos and videos auto-stamped with date, time, GPS coordinates. Blockchain hash prevents tampering.

3. Beneficiary Verification

Biometric or Aadhaar-linked attendance for beneficiary tracking (schools, health camps, skill training).

4. Real-Time Dashboards

CSR teams see live maps, attendance rates, milestone progress. Automated alerts for anomalies.
